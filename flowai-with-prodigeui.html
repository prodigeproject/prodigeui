Questions

What CTOs ask before they switch

How does FlowAI keep the roadmap current?

It connects to your issue tracker and repositories, then reads state changes as they land. When a task slips or scope grows, dependent milestones re-sequence automatically — no manual dragging required.

Does it replace our standup?

Most teams retire the daily sync within two weeks. FlowAI posts a written summary of movement, blockers, and dependencies each morning — the async record replaces the meeting rather than adding to it.

How accurate is the risk radar?

It models velocity, review latency, and scope drift against your own history. Teams see date accuracy improve as it learns their cadence — typically within the first sprint or two.

Is our code and project data secure?

FlowAI reads metadata, not source. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and Enterprise plans add SSO, audit logs, and configurable data residency.
