# Generative Social Proof & Testimonial Grid Guide (ProdigeUI)

> **Target Use-Case**: High-Converting Landing Pages, SaaS, Agency Portfolios.  
> ⚠️ **Strict Anti-Slop Rule**: Do NOT copy fake testimonial text or generic star ratings. Synthesize customer proof cards with authentic domain metrics.

---

## 1. Intent & Structure

- **Asymmetric Bento Social Proof**: Combine customer quotes with concrete outcome metrics (`-42% Cloud Spend`, `10x Faster Build Time`, `99.99% Uptime`).
- **Material Depth**: Cards feature subtle top inset highlights (`box-shadow: inset 0 1px 0 0 rgba(255,255,255,0.1)`), avatar portraits with explicit dimensions, and verified company tags.

---

## 2. Guardrails

- **Zero Prompt Leaks**: Prohibit placeholders like `"Lorem ipsum"` or `"[Author Name]"`.
- **Accessibility**: Full keyboard nav and high-contrast quote text.
