# High-Craft UI/UX Copywriting & Domain Storytelling System (ProdigeUI)

> ⚠️ **Copywriting is 50% of UX Craft.** High-craft interfaces look fake when paired with generic AI filler text ("Lorem ipsum", "Discover our solutions", "Get started now") or leaked prompt instructions (`(CLICK TO SWITCH PHOTO)`).

---

## 1. Non-Negotiable Copywriting Guardrails

### ❌ PROHIBITED (AI Slop Copywriting)
1. **No Prompt Instruction Leaks**: Never print UI instructions or click directives in micro-copy (e.g. `(CLICK TO SWITCH ROOM PHOTO)`, `(PRESS BUTTON TO SEE DATA)`).
2. **No Abstract Template Placeholders**: Never leave raw template brackets in output (e.g. `{NODE 01}`, `{Display Title}`, `{Tab 02}`).
3. **No Generic Tech Jargon Bloat**: Avoid empty buzzword soup ("Synergistic next-gen AI paradigm shift").
4. **No Filler Subheads**: Never write generic subheads like "Our Features", "Services", "Why Choose Us". Use narrative subheads (e.g. "Structures born of geology & light", "PR intelligence that protects sprint delivery").

### ✅ MANDATORY (High-Craft Authentic Copywriting)
1. **Authentic Domain Tone**: Speak with the authority of an industry leader:
   - *Architectural Studio*: "High-altitude stone residence constructed from locally quarried granite and European larch timber."
   - *DevOps SaaS*: "FlowAI reads pull requests, tickets, owners, and decisions—then shows which change protects the sprint."
   - *Creative Studio*: "We build brand experiences that disrupt markets."
2. **Precise Micro-Copy Telemetry**: Use concrete metrics and specific domain terms:
   - `58dB Structural Acoustic Mass`
   - `+4.2kW Winter Passive Solar Gain`
   - `PR #1084 API Schema Shift`
3. **Restrained Editorial Rhythm**: Keep paragraphs under 3 lines. Give headings room to breathe.
