# Market Reference Calibration

> A design system must learn from the market it is entering without copying a
> reference site's identity, wording, assets, interaction choreography, or
> geometry. The purpose of a reference read is to understand what the user is
> already trained to recognize, what evidence the category demands, and where a
> product can earn a distinctive move.

This document is mandatory in Creative Mode after Product Experience Architecture
and before art direction, layout, tokens, or media are selected. It is a guard
against the failure mode where every product is treated as a tasteful landing
page with a large headline, a split panel, and a palette variation.

## 1. Read the market, then name the difference

For every materially new domain, review two or more current, real product or
service experiences. Prefer the product's own site, app, purchase flow, service
guide, or help centre over galleries and trend roundups. A reference can tell us
about expected information, task entry, proof, commitment, and recovery; it must
not be used as a license to copy visual expression.

Record a private Market Reference Read:

| Field | Required decision |
| --- | --- |
| Domain and buying/usage context | What is the visitor actually trying to decide, start, compare, recover from, or complete? |
| Real references | At least two direct URLs, the page/flow observed, and the date observed. |
| Familiar entry pattern | What task, control, product object, search, or proof earns the first viewport? |
| Evidence grammar | What kind of evidence is credible here: live product state, physical product, location, case work, plan, provider, cost, eligibility, or progress? |
| Commitment and risk | What has to be transparent before commitment: money, fit, availability, consent, identity, timing, technical compatibility, or safety? |
| Visual grammar | Density, media hierarchy, type behavior, movement, and page topology that support the task. Describe function, never brand colors or copy. |
| Market convention to preserve | Which familiar pattern protects comprehension or trust? |
| Product-specific departure | Where should this product differ because its mechanism, audience, or posture differs? |
| Rejected generic fallback | Name the inappropriate pattern to avoid, e.g. `giant serif thesis plus framed two-column panel`. |

If live references are unavailable, record that limitation and use a restrained,
task-first structure rather than substituting a generic aesthetic.

## 2. Choose an experience archetype before a layout family

Category labels are too coarse. Select one primary archetype from the actual user
job and write the reason it fits. Blend only when the journey genuinely requires
two modes, and state which one owns the first viewport.

| Archetype | The first viewport earns attention with | Typical evidence and interaction | Do not reduce it to |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Operational control surface | A current decision, record, alert, queue, or work object | Ownership, time, status, action, history, and recovery | A marketing headline over decorative fake UI |
| Product exploration / high-consideration purchase | The real object plus the next buying question | Configuration, availability, cost/range/fit, comparison, booking, ownership support | An inspirational car or house poster with a generic form below |
| Editorial commerce / collection | A collection or product relationship with a clear route into selection | Category, material/fit/taste, price or availability when known, product imagery, search/filter/choice | A lifestyle hero that hides the product set |
| Trust and care navigation | The user's immediate need or constraint | Location, eligibility, urgency, provider, appointment, consent, safety, escalation | A warm brand promise with a buried care path |
| Place, visit, or cultural programme | The programme plus practical visit readiness | Date, ticket, access, map, exhibitions, arrival context, sensory/physical constraints | A museum poster that omits how a visitor gets through the day |
| Work-led company or studio | A real body of work and a credible point of view | Case work, discipline/sector filter, method, inquiry, team/context | A manifesto with anonymous decorative art |
| Learning practice loop | The next small, meaningful exercise | Prompt, action, feedback, progress, return/lapse state, evidence of skill | A course catalog or streak dashboard with no learning moment |
| Travel planning | A place plus the traveller's decisive constraints | Where/when/who, availability, route, local context, plan changes, cancellation/support | A scenic poster with a detached booking widget |

The selected archetype controls page topology. It is not a theme preset.

## 3. What current references actually show

These observations are deliberately structural, not recipes to imitate.

| Reference | Observed product behavior | Transferable lesson |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [SSENSE](https://www.ssense.com/) | Category choices and product/editorial imagery lead immediately; the store and its cultural layer are both first-class. | Fashion commerce needs product/collection hierarchy and an obvious route into selection, not a generic luxury statement. |
| [Linear](https://linear.app/) | The proposition is immediately backed by a dense, legible product state that carries real work nouns and system behavior. | Operational SaaS must show the object of work and the decision it improves; UI evidence is part of the story, not a decorative card. |
| [Polestar](https://www.polestar.com/us/) | Vehicle media is paired with shopping, test-drive, configuration, ownership, and lifestyle-cost tools. | High-consideration products need the object plus the uncertainty-reducing tool nearest the decision, not an abstract “premium” composition. |
| [Mercury](https://mercury.com/business-banking) | A concise banking proposition is followed directly by the actual account surface, with onboarding and trust context visible. | Fintech needs a controlled balance of familiar action, authentic system evidence, and plainly located safeguards. |
| [One Medical](https://www.onemedical.com/) | Care is organised around immediate needs, location, access, coverage, provider, and appointment expectations. | Trust-critical care must route by need and remove uncertainty before selling personality. |
| [Airbnb](https://www.airbnb.com/) | Destination, date, and guest inputs occupy the primary entry path; browsing follows that commitment. | Travel starts from a concrete planning question, not from imagery alone. |
| [The Modern House](https://themodernhouse.com/) | Listings are surrounded by editorial context about how a place is lived in; discovery prioritises current availability. | Property discovery benefits from lived evidence, narrative context, and availability-aware action—not a uniform listing card. |
| [MoMA visit information](https://www.moma.org/visit/tips) | Visit, ticket, accessibility, arrival, and in-building expectations are detailed as part of the cultural experience. | Cultural sites must make practical access and anxiety reduction first-class, alongside programme and art. |
| [Blue Bottle Coffee subscriptions](https://bluebottlecoffee.com/us/eng/subscriptions) | The offer teaches the choice sequence—coffee, cadence, and flexibility—while proving product quality and cancellation/change behavior. | Subscription commerce should make the choice model and post-purchase flexibility visible before requiring commitment. |
| [Pentagram](https://www.pentagram.com/) | Actual work dominates; a concise discipline/sector proposition provides a focused route into the body of evidence. | A company/studio earns its visual identity through real work and specificity, not a sweeping claim plus abstract shapes. |
| [Duolingo's teaching method](https://blog.duolingo.com/duolingo-teaching-method/) | Learning progresses from simple pattern recognition to harder application and adapts practice to what needs reinforcement. | Learning products need a credible practice/feedback/return loop rather than only a curriculum overview. |

## 4. Visual calibration: derive a grammar, do not inherit a house style

Before implementation, answer the following explicitly:

1. **What is the dominant proof?** A physical product, place, live task, case work,
   course exercise, date/availability control, provider, or a financial record.
   It must be visible early and have a job beyond decoration.
2. **What is the compositional engine?** Product grid, working surface, search
   form, full-bleed object, editorial sequence, itinerary, ticket flow, or
   practice loop. The engine may use a headline, but a headline cannot replace it.
3. **What type behavior matches the market?** Choose display, body, metadata, and
   data roles from the content. A large serif is a deliberate editorial or
   material decision, not a default signifier of quality.
4. **What density is honest?** Workflows and comparison need information density;
   a product or place may need visual breathing room; safety and access need calm
   legibility. Do not make every category sparse or every product dashboard-like.
5. **Where does the user act?** Put the truthful action beside the proof that
   makes it safe—not as a final, detached conversion band.
6. **What is the state after action?** Show what is retained, what may change,
   and how the user continues or repairs the flow.

## 5. Identity-collapse test

Before an artifact can pass review, compare it to the previous unrelated artifact.
Fail or flag when four or more of these remain unchanged without a product reason:

- first-viewport topology (for example, nav + oversized headline + split card);
- display-family genre, scale, and hierarchy;
- palette temperature and contrast posture;
- media role, crop, and scale;
- density and alignment system;
- action placement and control type;
- section sequence and closing behavior.

The goal is not forced novelty. It is to prevent the same visual grammar from
masquerading as ten products. A layout is valid only if its form can be explained
by the particular user's task, the market's evidence standards, and the product's
mechanism.

## 6. Fresh-generation benchmark protocol

For a benchmark across domains:

- create one independent Market Reference Read per case before drawing it;
- attach the chosen archetype, dominant proof, first-viewport composition, and
  rejected fallback to that case's audit record;
- use independently authored pages and media plans; do not use a shared page
  shell, shared CSS, generator, or canned section order;
- audit desktop and compact viewports against the reference-read decisions, not
  merely visual polish;
- include a cross-case comparison that states where the pages deliberately differ
  and which differences were a product decision;
- never claim that a small benchmark proves universal quality or user preference.

## Source limitations

These live references are a calibration set observed on 22 August 2026. They are
not visual templates, endorsements, or a substitute for research with the target
product's actual users. Product pages, pricing, and campaigns change; the durable
lesson is to inspect the live category before choosing a form.
