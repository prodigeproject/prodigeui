FAQ

Common questions

How does FlowAI integrate with our existing tools?

FlowAI connects natively with GitHub, GitLab, Jira, Linear, Slack, and Notion via OAuth. Setup takes under 5 minutes. Our API also supports custom integrations for internal tools.

Is our codebase data used to train your models?

Never. Your data is encrypted at rest and in transit, processed in isolated environments, and never used for model training. We're SOC 2 Type II certified and offer on-prem deployment for Enterprise plans.

How long until we see measurable results?

Most teams see a 20-30% improvement in sprint predictability within the first two sprints. The AI model improves continuously as it learns your team's patterns and codebase structure.

Can FlowAI work with teams across multiple timezones?

Absolutely. Timezone-aware task routing is a core feature. FlowAI optimizes handoff points, async communication windows, and review assignments to minimize waiting time across distributed teams.
