Before you connect.

Does FlowAI replace our issue tracker?

No. It adds a decision layer above the tools your team already uses and links every insight back to its source.

Which tools can we connect?

The benchmark concept includes GitHub, Linear, Jira, Slack, and common CI providers. Confirm the production integration list before publishing.

Is our source code used to train shared models?

The intended product policy is workspace isolation and no shared-model training. This security claim requires legal and technical confirmation.

Can we reverse an AI recommendation?

Yes. Recommendations remain suggestions until an accountable owner accepts them, and the interface exposes source, consequence, and reversal where applicable.
