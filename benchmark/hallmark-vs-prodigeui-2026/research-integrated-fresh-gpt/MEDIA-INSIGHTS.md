# Media-derived tuning notes

The supplied screenshot and video were treated as critique evidence, not as a template.

## Preserved principles

- **Visual mass needs a counterweight.** The screenshot makes imbalance visible even when
  widths are numerically similar; apparent weight comes from area, contrast, shape, and
  proximity, not only grid math.
- **Whitespace is active.** The quiet field around the hero gives the focal mark room to
  read and makes the comparison legible. Empty space must frame, pace, or clarify.
- **One accent action is enough.** The yellow CTA is effective because it is scarce and
  clearly separated from neutral navigation and body copy. The system should preserve the
  scarcity principle, not the hue.
- **Proof follows proposition.** The video repeatedly pairs the headline with a legible
  product/interface proof surface soon after the proposition; this is stronger than a
  decorative hero with evidence deferred below.
- **Soft contrast can still be decisive.** Light neutrals, restrained borders, and one dark
  text family can feel premium when scale and spacing do the hierarchy work.

## Explicitly not promoted

- the exact blue geometric mark;
- a two-column split or symmetric object grouping;
- the yellow color value;
- the product category, labels, or testimonial content visible in the video;
- a universal hero sequence or a prescribed screenshot scale.
