The studio

Six people who sweat the details you will never consciously notice.

We believe the difference between good and great is a thousand small decisions: the weight of a shadow, the timing of a transition, the word on a button. We make those decisions on purpose.

Founded in 2016, NOVA works with founders, cultural institutions, and product teams who want work that lasts longer than a trend cycle. We take on a handful of projects a year so each one gets our full attention.
