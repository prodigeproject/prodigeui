FAQ

Questions CTOs ask first.

How does the risk score work?+

It reads PR state, review latency, CI results and past velocity on similar work, then scores on-time confidence per item. Every score links to the signals behind it, so you can check the reasoning rather than trust a black box.1

What does it connect to?+

GitHub, GitLab, Linear, Jira and Google Calendar today. It reads activity to plan and never writes to your repos without an explicit action you approve.

Is our code and data safe?+

FlowAI reads metadata (PR titles, states, timings), not source by default. SOC 2 Type II is in progress; SSO, SAML and an audit log ship on Pro and Enterprise.3

How long to see value?+

Connect a repo and the first roadmap and risk read are ready in minutes. Most teams drop their standup meeting within the first two weeks.2
