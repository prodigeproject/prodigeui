## Transfer meaning

- FlowAI sets the operational standard: a cohesive evidence-backed decision artifact in the
  first viewport, technical-human display typography, calm reading and metadata roles, varied
  section rhythm, and closing color continuity.
- NOVA v2 remains the expressive standard: poster-scale identity, differentiated display,
  editorial, and UI roles, a concept-tied focal artwork, owned caption contrast, and varied
  editorial composition.
- The rejected equal-card NOVA experiment is not part of the standard. Its forced aligned
  project framing is now an explicit expressive-profile anti-pattern. A fresh reproduction
  must instead preserve proposition-led scale, concept artwork, varied work fields, owned
  captions, and the alternation between poster volume and editorial breathing room.
- The canonical `oneBuildPreflight` now treats the first emitted implementation as the
  acceptance candidate. It pre-resolves contrast pairs, font and weight budgets, 44x44
  targets, subject scale, responsive containment, reduced motion, deterministic assets, and
  encoding. The executable gate remains final proof rather than permission for a known-bad
  draft-and-repair workflow.
