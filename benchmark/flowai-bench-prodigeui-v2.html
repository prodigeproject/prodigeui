Questions engineering leaders ask.

How does FlowAI know our real progress? +

It connects to GitHub/GitLab and your issue tracker, reads commit, PR, and issue activity, and maps it to your plan. No manual status updates required.

Do engineers have to change how they work? +

No. FlowAI observes the tools you already use. There's nothing new to update — that's the point.

Is our code or data used for training? +

Never. Your repository metadata stays in your workspace, and FlowAI is SOC 2 Type II with a zero-retention model option on Enterprise.

How long does setup take? +

About ten minutes: connect your Git provider, pick the repos, and FlowAI backfills the last 90 days to build your first forecast.
