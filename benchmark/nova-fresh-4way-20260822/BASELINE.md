# NOVA baseline decision — v3.0.8

Selected baseline: `2ab4e19` / `nova-2ab4e19.html`
Decision date: 22 August 2026
Status: benchmark baseline only; no historical Git commit or source snapshot has been overwritten.

## Why this is the baseline

`2ab4e19` scored 92/100 in the manual review and is the most balanced expression of the NOVA brief. Its strongest mechanism is the relationship between a large editorial headline, a single dark monolith, and an intentionally quiet paper field. The composition feels authored before it feels decorative.

## Preserve exactly

- The paper / ink / coral / lilac surface family and the restrained number of accents.
- A single dominant hero artifact with a clear counterweight to the headline.
- Display serif for the proposition, neutral sans for reading/actions, mono only for annotation.
- The asymmetric but calm section rhythm and decisive dark closing chapter.
- The existing 26-pass, 0-fail desktop/mobile technical baseline.

## Improvement backlog

### P0 — make the hero prove more

Replace or evolve the geometric monolith into one locally stored, provenance-recorded studio artifact: a real campaign crop, a photographed material object, an original image, or an authored work sample. It must remain one focal subject, not become a collage of unrelated media. The strongest learning from `e6c1acd` is media integration, not simply adding an image.

### P0 — make project evidence specific

The current project names and one-line descriptions are credible but generic. Give each selected project a distinct medium and proof: one identity application, one spatial/editorial object, and one digital interaction state. Vary the project geometry according to the evidence rather than equal card convention.

### P1 — sharpen the proposition and closing

Replace broad studio language with one claim that only NOVA could make, then let the closing CTA resolve that same claim. Keep the original palette continuity; do not add a new closing hue or a thin utility-style footer.

### P1 — validate editorial rhythm below the fold

Retain the hero's quiet field, but make every large section earn its area through artwork, evidence, interaction, or a meaningful chapter change. Audit desktop and mobile silhouettes for accidental blank bands.

### P2 — add motion only when it carries meaning

The static baseline is already strong. If motion is added, use one short, reduced-motion-safe transition that reveals the monolith/material relationship or a project state. Do not add ambient glow, particle fields, or a generic signal engine.

## Next-candidate acceptance criteria

- Keep the v3.0.8 compositional thesis recognisable without copying its geometry literally.
- Raise `Artifak/media` from 6/10 to at least 8/10 through one integrated local asset.
- Raise `Art direction` and `Rhythm` to at least 19/20 and 15/15 respectively.
- Preserve zero automated gate failures at desktop and mobile.
- Score higher than 92 only if the added evidence improves specificity without weakening the quiet editorial hierarchy.

## Explicit non-goals

- Do not imitate the orbit, acid archive, cobalt-yellow poster, or HSL signal-engine variants.
- Do not replace the restrained serif-led hierarchy with oversized all-caps display type.
- Do not use benchmark HTML as a code or layout template for the next candidate.
