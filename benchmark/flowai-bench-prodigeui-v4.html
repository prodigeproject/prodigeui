Frequently asked questions

How does FlowAI sync with our existing tools?
FlowAI integrates natively with GitHub, GitLab, Linear, Jira, and Slack. Setup takes under 5 minutes — connect your repos, and the roadmap starts populating automatically from your existing workflow.
Is our source code ever stored or read?
No. FlowAI reads metadata (commit messages, PR titles, ticket status) but never accesses or stores your source code. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest.
Can we try FlowAI with one team before rolling out?
Absolutely. Most companies start with a single squad on the Team plan and expand from there. There are no minimum seat requirements.
What happens when our trial ends?
You keep read-only access to all data generated during the trial. Upgrade anytime to restore full functionality — no data is ever deleted.
