# Landing Page Testimonials / Social Proof — Sub-Guide

> Parent: [use-cases/landing/guide.md](./guide.md)

## Recommended Components
- **Card** — Testimonial quote cards
- **Text** — Quote content, attribution name and role
- **Badge** — Company or role labels
- **Carousel / Slider** — Testimonial rotation (optional)
- **Image** — Avatar or company logo

## Tokens
- `typography.body.lg` — quote text (italic style)
- `typography.body.sm` — attribution text
- `color.text.primary` — quote content
- `color.text.secondary` — attribution name and role
- `color.surface.secondary` — card background
- `space.lg` — gap between testimonial cards
- `radius.md` — card corners
- `border.subtle` — card subtle border

## Motion Presets
- `enter-exit` — cards fade in on scroll (staggered)
- `state-transition` — carousel slide transitions
- `scroll-reveal` — section reveal animation

## Patterns
- Show 2-3 visible testimonials at once; carousel for more
- Include name, role/title, company, and avatar for credibility
- Pull quotes: use larger typography with quotation marks styling
- Company logos row below testimonials for additional social proof
- Keep quotes concise (2-3 sentences max) for readability
- Mix testimonial types: text quotes, video thumbnails, metric callouts
- On mobile: stack cards vertically or use swipeable carousel
- Ensure avatar images have alt text with person's name
