Frequently Asked Questions

How does FlowAI connect to our repos securely?

FlowAI uses OAuth read-only tokens for GitHub, GitLab, and Bitbucket. We only process metadata in-memory — your source code is never stored on our servers.

Does FlowAI replace Jira or Linear?

No, FlowAI connects directly to Jira, Linear, and GitHub Issues to sync progress bidirectionally and remove manual tracking overhead.

Can we deploy FlowAI inside our private VPC?

Yes! Enterprise customers receive a Helm chart for private Kubernetes/VPC deployment with full air-gap support.

How fast can we set up our team?

Setup takes less than 3 minutes. Authenticate your git provider, select your repositories, and FlowAI immediately renders your sprint risk telemetry.
